Part A - Minimum Standard Conditions (apply to every WO/PO)
1. Acceptance. Supplier's signed copy, written confirmation, or WhatsApp confirmation of this PO constitutes acceptance of all its conditions. Commencing supply also constitutes acceptance.
2. Prices firm. Rates are firm for the full order quantity and duration; no escalation is payable unless agreed in writing as a Special Condition.
3. Delivery & delay. Delivery by the date stated is essential. For delay attributable to the supplier, buyer may deduct liquidated damages of 0.5% of the delayed portion's value per week, up to a maximum of 5%, and/or cancel the unexecuted portion.
4. Quality & rejection. Goods must conform to the specification, sample (if any), and quality documents promised. Rejected goods will be returned at supplier's risk and cost, including to-and-fro freight; replacement or refund within 15 days.
5. Invoicing & GST. A GST-compliant tax invoice quoting this PO number must accompany the goods, along with promised test certificates. Payment of the GST portion is conditional on the invoice appearing in buyer's GSTR-2B; amounts may be withheld if the supplier fails to file.
6. Risk & title. Risk in the goods passes to the buyer only on receipt AND acceptance at the delivery location; title passes on payment or acceptance, whichever is earlier.
7. Compliance & origin data. Supplier shall comply with all applicable laws (including labour and environmental laws, and prohibition of child labour) and shall, on request, declare the origin of materials and provide data reasonably required for buyer's export compliance (e.g., CBAM emissions data, EUDR origin/geo-location, product composition for Digital Product Passports) at no extra charge.
